Titanic — survival rate by group

Toggle the grouping variable and watch which pattern the data actually reveals.

Every gap you see here is a candidate feature. A ~52-point gap between sexes, a ~46-point gap between first and third class, and children clearly outperforming adults are exactly the signals a logistic regression or random forest (Part 1 of the tutorial) will end up leaning on most heavily — discovering them here, visually, before training anything, is what makes the model's later feature-importance output feel like confirmation rather than a surprise.

This is also why a single overall survival rate would have hidden all three stories. One combined number (~45% survived overall in this dataset) is the average of several very different sub-stories layered on top of each other — exactly the reasoning the EDA tutorial's Simpson's Paradox section warns never to skip.

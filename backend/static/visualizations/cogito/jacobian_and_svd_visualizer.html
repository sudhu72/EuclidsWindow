The Softmax Jacobian, and Why the Gradient Is p − y

Scores z = (2.0, 1.0, 0.1) go through softmax, then cross-entropy. Each button plays one fully worked step of the tutorial. Every number is computed live; nothing to type.

How to read the grid: the cell in row pᵢ, column zⱼ is ∂pᵢ/∂zⱼ, how much probability i moves when score j is nudged. Teal cells are the diagonal (pᵢ(1 − pᵢ)); coral cells are off-diagonal (−pᵢ·pⱼ).

The Pseudo-Inverse: Undo Every Stretch You Can, Leave Crushed Directions Blank

What's happening: A⁺ = VΣ⁺Uᵀ undoes SVD's three moves in reverse, dividing by every non-zero singular value and putting 0 wherever a direction was crushed. The answer x⁺ = A⁺b fits b as well as anything can, and among all the answers tied for that best fit it's the shortest. Every number here is computed live.

Try this: on x + 2y = 5, drag the candidate along the solution line and watch its length never drop below 2.2361, the length of x⁺. On Gradient descent, compare the two starting points. On Tiny σ blow-up, press ε = 0.001 and watch a 0.01 nudge in b throw the answer to (−8, 10).

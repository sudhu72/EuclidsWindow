RMSNorm vs. LayerNorm

LayerNorm: (x − mean) / √var. RMSNorm skips the mean: x / √mean(x²). They agree whenever a row's mean is already 0. ε is left out, as in the tutorial's numbers.

Batch Norm: train mode vs. eval mode

Train mode normalizes with the current batch's own mean and variance. Eval mode uses running statistics, updated during training as running = 0.9 · running + 0.1 · batch (momentum 0.1), starting from mean 0, variance 1. Population variance, ε = 10⁻⁵.

Where this connects: unit vectors (linear algebra) are "divide by the vector's own length" — the same recipe with the vector's length as the scale reference. Probabilities summing to 1 (probability) are "fraction of total." Softmax is exactly what turns a network's raw output scores into the probability distribution that cross-entropy needs as input. Combinatorics is not the same toolkit — it counts arrangements; normalization is the separate step of turning any raw values (counts or otherwise) into comparable form. The one place they meet: dividing a combinatorial count by a total count is precisely how counting becomes probability.

The five lenses

Every technique below is that one recipe, with a different reference point and scale plugged in. Drag any value and watch all five panels move together — same colored bar, same position, in every chart.

Where this connects: unit vectors (Part 1) are "divide by the vector's own length" — the same recipe with the vector's norm as the scale reference. Probabilities summing to 1 (Part 3) are "fraction of total." Softmax is exactly what turns a network's raw output scores into the probability distribution that cross-entropy (Part 6) needs as input. Combinatorics is not the same toolkit — it counts arrangements; normalization is the separate step of turning any raw values (counts or otherwise) into comparable form. The one place they meet: dividing a combinatorial count by a total count is precisely how counting becomes probability.

Residual connections keep gradients alive

Companion to §3.2 of the Jacobian & J-space tutorial. A real simulation runs in your browser: a stack of tanh layers, 64 units each, weights drawn from N(0, scale²/64) with a seeded random generator (so every click gives the same numbers), one N(0, 1) input vector. A gradient of size 1 starts at the top and is backpropagated to the input, through a plain network (y = F(x)) and a residual one (y = x + F(x)). Your numbers sit next to the tutorial's; they differ a little because the random draws differ. Every example is one click.

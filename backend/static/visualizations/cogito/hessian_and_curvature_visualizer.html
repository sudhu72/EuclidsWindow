The Shape of the Ground

The gradient says which way is downhill. The Hessian says what shape the ground is: a bowl, a flat-floored valley, or a saddle. Every worked example from the file is a one-click preset below, and every number is computed live on this page.

Five matrices, five shapes of xᵀAx

Each button draws the height xᵀAx over the plane. Teal shading is above zero, red is below zero. The dashed lines are the eigenvector directions, labelled with their eigenvalues (how steeply the surface curves that way). The three dots are the test points (1,1), (1,−1) and (1,0).

Height, then tilt, then bend

f(x, y) = eˣ + x·y² at p = (0, 1), stepping h = (0.1, 0.1). The prediction is f(p) + ∇f(p)ᵀh + ½·hᵀHh, built up one term at a time. The top plot slices the surface along the direction of h (the step lands at t = 1). The bottom plot shows each error on a log scale, so tiny errors stay visible.

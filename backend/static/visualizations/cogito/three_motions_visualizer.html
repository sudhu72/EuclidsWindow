Where this connects: the eigenvector directions here are the same "stretch, don't rotate" idea from Part 1.2 — try the shear preset and notice only one direction survives. The tangent line is Part 2's "local straight-line approximation," and the Riemann sum is literally the construction behind the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus. The direction field traces exactly the "leaf on a river of arrows" picture from Part 8 — Euler's method is that picture, made computational, one tiny step at a time.

Memorylessness — the bus that never learns

Two kinds of "waiting for the bus," both averaging 10 minutes. Only one of them has no memory of how long you've already waited.

already waited 9 minutes (further wait threshold T is fixed at 5 minutes)
fresh arrival: P(wait > 5 min)
–
already waited S: P(wait > S+5 | wait > S)
–

Exponential arrivals have no schedule to be "due" against. By construction, every instant carries the exact same tiny chance of the bus arriving, regardless of history — that's the one assumption that generates the exponential shape in the first place, so conditioning on "already waited S" changes nothing about what happens next.

Scheduled-ish arrivals carry real information in how long you've waited. If the bus is known to arrive somewhere in a fixed window and it hasn't shown up after most of that window has elapsed, it's now "due" — the odds of a long further wait actually drop, exactly the opposite of memorylessness.

Drag S toward 15 on the scheduled-ish setting and watch the conditional probability keep falling — the closer you get to the end of the window, the more certain the bus becomes. The exponential setting's conditional probability stays flat at every S, no matter how high you drag it.

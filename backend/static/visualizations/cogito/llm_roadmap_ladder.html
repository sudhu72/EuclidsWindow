The spine that connects all eight

Every pillar answers the same question at a bigger budget: “the model is fixed — how do I get better behavior without retraining it, and when that fails, how do I retrain the smallest possible piece?” Change the words → change the facts → budget the attention → edit a sliver of weights → let it act in a loop → wrap it in infrastructure → shrink it → prove it works. The most common mistake in the whole field is skipping a rung.

Watch fit() and transform() reshape a bell curve

200 training exam scores and 40 test scores, both roughly bell-shaped. Scrub the slider (or press play) and watch the whole distribution slide as the mean is subtracted, then narrow — and grow taller, to keep the same total area — as the standard deviation is divided out, converging exactly onto the standard normal curve.

Byte-Pair Encoding, step by step

Companion to tokenization_bpe_feynman.md. BPE splits every word into characters plus an end-of-word mark </w>, counts every neighbouring pair (weighted by how often the word occurs), glues the most frequent pair into one new symbol, and repeats. Ties go to the first pair found, scanning the words in order and each word left to right (as in the paper's code). Everything below is computed live in your browser. Every worked example is one click.

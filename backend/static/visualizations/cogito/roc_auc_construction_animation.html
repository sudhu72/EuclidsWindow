The ROC curve this classifier actually traces

x = FPR, y = TPR  ·  dashed diagonal = random guessing  ·  shaded area = AUC

Geometric AUC: the trapezoid-rule area under the staircase above, using every one of the 10 emails' own scores as a threshold stop, comes out to exactly 0.80.

Probabilistic AUC: compare all 5×5 = 25 possible (spam email, not-spam email) pairs. Count how often the spam email's score is higher (a tie would count as half). For this dataset: 20 / 25 = 0.80 — no ties, and an exact match with the geometric number above, because they are the same fact computed two different ways: AUC is the probability a random positive outranks a random negative.

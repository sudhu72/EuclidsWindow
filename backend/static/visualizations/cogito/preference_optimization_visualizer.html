How RLHF actually works

Companion to §3.8.1 of the Feynman ML/DL/LLM tutorial. Four pieces turn a next-word predictor into an assistant: supervised fine-tuning, a reward model trained on "A is better than B", an RL step held on a KL leash (or DPO, which folds the last two into one loss), and GRPO's group-relative scoring for checkable answers. Every number below is computed live from the formulas and matches the tutorial's tables. Every worked example is one click.

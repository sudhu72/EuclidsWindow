Naive Bayes: watching the evidence pile up

Two tabs, matching the two worked examples in naive_bayes_feynman.md: a word-by-word multinomial spam filter with real Laplace smoothing, and a Gaussian Naive Bayes decision boundary computed live from a small numeric dataset. Everything below is computed in this page, live, in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

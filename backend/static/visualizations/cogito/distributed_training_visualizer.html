Ring all-reduce, one step at a time

Each GPU passes one chunk to its right-hand neighbour per step. First N−1 steps (reduce-scatter): the receiver adds the chunk to its own. Next N−1 steps (all-gather): the receiver overwrites its copy with the finished sum.

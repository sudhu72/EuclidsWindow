The Test That Fools Doctors

A rare disease, an excellent test, a positive result. Drag the sliders and watch P(sick | positive) defy your intuition — because the base rate, not the test, runs the show. Ten thousand imaginary people flow down the tree.

Multiply down each path to get its leaf; add the two positive leaves to get everyone who tested positive; then part ÷ whole gives the reversed probability. That reversal is Bayes' theorem — the tree is the theorem.

Activation slopes & saturation

Companion to §2.4.2 of the Feynman ML/DL/LLM tutorial. Backprop multiplies the blame by each activation's own local slope: σ'(z) = σ(z)(1 − σ(z)), tanh'(z) = 1 − tanh²(z), and ReLU's slope is 1 for z > 0 and 0 for z < 0. Every number below is computed live from those formulas and checked against the tutorial's table. Every worked example is one click.
